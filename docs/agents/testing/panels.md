# Testing: text panels, images and the fold

The text panels, their images, the fold and the Last watched line. The index is `docs/agents/testing.md`.

- `tests/fold.spec.ts` (a light spec) opens MediaSafe, Off the keyboard and Home lab at 1280×720, 1440×900 and 1920×1080 and checks the "more" cue (`data-more`) isn't showing, the panel's `scrollHeight - clientHeight` is at most 4, the cue's own threshold (`src/text/more.ts`), and its `scrollWidth - clientWidth` is at most 4. It waits for every `img` in the panel to load or fail first. A page is one entry in its `PAGES` list: `answer` serves a fake MediaSafe answer (`page.route`, any-origin header, `text/plain; charset=utf-8`), waits for the Last watched line to show it, and also checks the line is one line high; `fixme` marks a known failure (none today). Off the keyboard has two entries, each with a title of `LAST_WATCHED_MAX` code points (from `src/last-watched.ts`): one of words, which would wrap, and one with no spaces, which would run off the side (#370). It no longer adds a line of its own. Break check: drop the line's one-line rule (`white-space: nowrap; overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis` on `.crt-last-watched` in `src/text/ScreenPanels.tsx`) and all six Off the keyboard entries fail, at 1280×720 too. A later ticket edits only its own page's entry
- `tests/links-row.spec.ts` (a light spec) checks a page with no links draws no links row (`data-links`: Why I do this and Off the keyboard) and a page with links draws one holding exactly its links (Contact, Software factory). The break check is to draw the row always
- `tests/mediasafe-image.spec.ts` (a light spec) checks the MediaSafe page's Library screenshot (#341): no request for it at boot, on another station or on another Projects page, and one once its page is shown; its box has the file's own size; at 1280×720 the heading, lead and stat are in the column left of it, 9.5em wide at the panel's right; at 390×844 it is after the bullets and the stat; on a short landscape window (667×375) it is neither shown nor fetched; and a file that fails to load shows nothing. `tests/fold.spec.ts` checks the fold with the image loaded
- `tests/golf-photo.spec.ts` (a light spec) checks Off the keyboard's golf day photo (#342): no request for it at boot, on another station or on another About me page, and one once its page is shown; its alt text, its caption as real text centred under it, and no button or dialog (it opens no larger); its box has the file's own size; it is after the bullets at 1280×720 and 390×844; on a short landscape window (667×375) neither it nor its caption is shown or fetched; a file that fails to load shows neither; and on a window too short for the page (1280×560) the cue shows while the photo is mounted and goes when the file fails. `src/page-html.test.ts` checks the caption is in the pre-boot HTML. `tests/fold.spec.ts`'s Off the keyboard entries serve an answer with the longest accepted title, so the fold is checked with the Last watched line in it (#370)
- `tests/image-larger.spec.ts` (a light spec) checks the MediaSafe screenshot opens larger (#344): a click, Enter and Space on its button each open a dialog named by the image's alt text with focus on Close, and Tab and Shift+Tab stay on it; Esc, Close and a click on the image each shut it and put focus back on the button; with it open Esc only shuts it (the visitor stays on the MediaSafe page, and the next Esc goes up); an arrow key, the browser's Back, a HUD link, the Next button and the breadcrumb's link each shut it and move (the dialog covers the whole visible panel, so a real click can't reach the last two: the test fires their click with `dispatchEvent`); a re-render of the tour (a pointer on a HUD link) with the dialog open leaves focus on the dialog (after a click on its empty glass) and the dialog's top where it was (at 1280×560, where the MediaSafe page overflows its panel; the test scrolls the panel by script and asserts it moved, since at 1280×720 the panel can't scroll and the top check would see nothing); a click on the open image doesn't go back to the overview; at 1280×720 and 390×844 the image is inside the panel and fills the dialog's width or height, also when the panel had scrolled; the portrait has no button; and with the screenshot's request answered 404 there is no image and no button. The break check for the re-render test: in `src/text/LargeImage.tsx` make the layout effect depend on `shot` again (not `width` and `height`) and run `-g re-render`; it fails on the focus check and, with that check commented out, on the top check. The break check for the Esc guard: change the Esc line in `src/TourView.tsx` back to a plain `visit.up()` and run `heavy npx playwright test tests/image-larger.spec.ts --workers=1 -g Esc`; the two Esc tests fail
- `src/last-watched.test.ts` tests the Last watched read with the fetch faked and a fake clock (as `src/gap.test.ts` does for the gap): a good answer sent as `text/plain; charset=utf-8`, the 5 s default timeout (4999 ms, 5000 ms), non-OK, failed and wrong-type answers, bodies that are empty, hold a line break, a control character or an invisible formatting character (U+202E, U+2066, U+200B, or only zero-width spaces; ordinary accented and non-Latin letters still pass), the 80-code-point cap swept at 0, 1, 80 and 81 with titles outside the Basic Multilingual Plane, the 512-byte body cap (512 accepted, 513 refused, 320 bytes of 4-byte characters accepted, a body that streams on forever refused without the clock moving), and `redirect: "error"` on the call. `src/last-watched.ts` imports nothing, so a browser spec can import `LAST_WATCHED_MAX` from it
- `tests/last-watched.spec.ts` (a light spec, run by the release's `browser-light` job after staging moves) checks the Last watched line (#369) with every answer faked by `page.route`: the line shows on Off the keyboard, under the watching bullet and aligned with its text, and on no other page; no call at boot or on any other page before Off the keyboard is shown, one call on a direct link to it at boot, and no second call after going away and back; with the answer failing (404) or no answer served the bullet shows and the line does not; `<b>x</b>` shows as those characters with no `b` element; the line shows at 390×844 and 667×375 with `getAnimations()` empty; an 80-character title with no spaces (built from `LAST_WATCHED_MAX`) at 390×844 is one line high, `nowrap` with `text-overflow: ellipsis` and wider than its box, and the panel's `scrollWidth - clientWidth` is at most 4. With the answer held back and then released, after lines are added under the bullets until the page is just under the cue's threshold, `data-more` is off before the answer and on after it, with no frame asked for in between (#370: the panel works the cue out again when the answer lands; break check: remove the `useLayoutEffect` on `lwAnswer` in `src/text/ScreenPanels.tsx` and it fails). It also shows the flag works: a fake still answers, and an unserved fetch to MediaSafe's host is refused. Break checks: start the read at mount (in `ScreenPanels`) and the no-call-before-shown test fails; show the line whatever the read gave and the two failure tests fail. Run it with `heavy npx playwright test tests/last-watched.spec.ts --workers=1`. `src/text/last-watched-store.test.ts` tests the store the panels read (one call however often it is started, a failed read shows nothing), `src/content.test.ts` the words and the bullet they name, and `src/page-html.test.ts` that the pre-boot HTML has no Last watched line
